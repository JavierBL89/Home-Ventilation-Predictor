🔍 Ventilation Time Prediction: Overview

App capable of predicting the the most optimal time to ventilate our home using three different forecasting models. Each model is trained with fake indoor and outdoor temperature fluctuations data of a whole year to analyze trends and generate future predictions.

How It Works

This app uses advanced machine learning models and weather data to suggest the best time to ventilate your home.

  1. Indoor temperature is forecasted using the TimesNet transformer model.
  2. Outdoor temperature forecasts are retrieved from the OpenWeather API.
  3. The app calculates the optimal ventilation time based on the season and temperature differences.

🔄 Logic Behind

Ventilation decision logic:

  1. Forecast indoor temperature (TimesNet).
  2. Interpolate outdoor forecast from OpenWeather API.
  3. Detect the hour with maximum difference:
    • In summer: ventilate during the coolest indoor vs. outdoor gap (e.g., late night).
    • In winter: ventilate during the warmest difference (e.g., midday).

This logic is season-aware, allowing the app to give different suggestions per season.

📕 TimesNet (Time-Series Transformer)

  • Type: Deep Learning Transformer
  • Best for: Complex, multi-step dependencies and long-term temporal patterns
  • Key Feature: Learns temporal dynamics with attention mechanisms

🔧 Workflow:

  1. Pretrained Model: We use a pretrained version of TimesNet hosted on Hugging Face. Our pipeline feeds it hourly indoor temperature sequences.

  2. Model Input: The past 24–48 hours of indoor temperatures are formatted into the model’s input format. Outdoor temperatures are interpolated as context (optional extension).

  3. Inference & Prediction: TimesNet outputs the next 48 indoor hourly predictions. We align these with the interpolated outdoor data to determine:
    • 🕐 Best Time
    • 🌅 Morning Option
    • 🌇 Evening Option
    • 🌀 Alternatives
